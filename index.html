About

I am a Member of Technical Staff at Mosi Intelligence (MOSI), focusing on performance optimization for large-scale training and inference systems. As part of the OpenMOSS team, I work on multimodal foundation models across image, video, audio and speech, spanning both understanding and generation.

Previously, I worked on large language model pre-training at InternLM (书生·浦语) in Shanghai AI Laboratory. I received my M.S. (2022) and B.S. (2019) degrees from the School of Computer Science and Technology at Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), advised by Prof. Peng Wang.

My research and engineering interests focus on:

  • Large-Scale Training Systems
  • High-Performance Inference Systems
  • Multimodal Understanding and Generation

We are hiring! We are looking for engineers and researchers interested in AI infrastructure, large-scale training/inference systems, and multimodal foundation models. Feel free to reach out if you are interested in joining us.

    One runtime for native full-duplex models, turn-based multimodal models and cascaded speech systems, built on composable graphs and persistent sessions. On a single H200, an ASR → LLM → TTS cascade reaches 0.17 s mean stop latency on interruption with 16 concurrent sessions.
